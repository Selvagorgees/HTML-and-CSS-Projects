Chocolate Chip Cookies

A simple recipe for soft and delicious chocolate chip cookies.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix the butter and sugar.
  3. Add the eggs and flour.
  4. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
